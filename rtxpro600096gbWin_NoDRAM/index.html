VRAM Hot/Cold L1 + Direct NVMe Streaming (0.30 GB RAM)

With --dram-cache-gb -1, Moecher uses 0.0 GB of host DRAM expert cache (only a 0.30 GB 32-slot pinned I/O staging ring — 16x less RAM than DwarfStar's 4.80 GB). It keeps 3,840 hot experts in NVFP4 (sm_120a) and 1,100 top cold experts in VRAM L1 (92.9 GB total VRAM), streaming uncached cold experts directly from NVMe SSD across 16 worker threads. Because Moecher's mixed-quantization cold experts use IQ2_XXS + Q2_K (9.48 MB per expert vs DwarfStar's 2.5 MB uniform IQ2_XXS), each cold SSD read transfers 3.8x more bytes, explaining why enabling Moecher's DDR5 L2 cache jumps performance from 103.12 → 189.53 tok/s prefill and 5.21 → 14.50 tok/s decode.

DwarfStar uses uniform IQ2_XXS (~2.5 MB/expert) across all experts and 4,096-token Batched Chunk GEMM.
